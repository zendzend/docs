Your [workspace](/key-concepts) is the shared environment that holds your spaces, pages, media, devices, and automations. This page covers the settings that apply to it and to your own view of the app.

## Workspace identity

When you first set up Fabl, the onboarding flow establishes your workspace:

- **Workspace name** — the display name for your team's environment
- **Workspace handle** — used in your workspace URL
- An optional **use-case profile** (what you'll use Fabl for and your setup type) that helps tailor onboarding

## Get-started checklist

New workspaces show a **Get started** checklist on the dashboard to guide initial setup. It tracks the key first steps:

- Set up your workspace
- Invite a teammate
- Connect a device
- Create your first rule

Each item links to where you complete it, and the checklist can be dismissed once you're set up.

## Appearance

Each member controls their own **appearance** from settings:

- **Theme** — Light, Dark, or System (which follows your operating system preference)

Your theme preference is saved to your account, so it follows you across devices and browsers — change it on one device and it updates everywhere you're signed in. Other personal view preferences, such as which sidebar sections are expanded and your recently-visited list, sync the same way. Device-specific toggles, like collapsing the sidebar, stay on each device.

## Members and permissions

Workspace membership and access are managed separately. See [Invite members](/team/invite-members) to add teammates and [Roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions) for how access is controlled.
