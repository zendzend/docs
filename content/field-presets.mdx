A **field preset** is a saved definition of a single [field](/content/fields) — its type, label, default value, options, formatting, and access mode. Presets let you standardize fields across pages instead of reconfiguring the same field every time.

## Why use presets

If your rundowns always include a "Segment duration" time field or a "Guest name" text field, define each once as a preset. Then anyone can add a correctly configured field in a single click, and your fields stay consistent across the whole workspace.

## Create and use a preset

1. Open the **Field presets** section and click **New field preset**.
2. Choose the field type and configure its label, default value, options, icon, and access mode.
3. On any page, use the **\+** control, pick **Field presets**, and select your preset to add a field configured from it.

A field added from a preset is independent: editing the field on a page does not change the preset, and changing the preset does not alter fields already placed on pages.

<Note>
  Creating and editing presets requires the field presets management permission. See [Roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions).
</Note>
