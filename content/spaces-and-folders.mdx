Spaces and folders are how you organize content inside your workspace. They appear in the left sidebar and give every page a home.

## Spaces

A **space** is a top-level container in your workspace. Spaces usually map to a show, a production, or a team, and they keep unrelated work cleanly separated. Each space holds pages and folders.

From the sidebar you can:

- **Create a space** to start a new area of work
- **Rename a space** or change its **icon**
- **Favorite a space** to pin it to the top of the sidebar
- **Expand or collapse** a space to show or hide its contents

Opening a space shows its pages along with filters for searching and narrowing by date or tag.

## Folders

A **folder** groups pages inside a space, and folders can be nested to any depth. Use them to organize by episode, segment, shooting day, or any structure that suits your team. Folders are optional — a page can live directly in a space.

From a space or folder you can:

- **Create a folder** and nest folders inside other folders
- **Move** pages and folders by dragging them in the sidebar
- **Rename** or **delete** a folder

<Note>
  Managing spaces and the workspace structure requires the workspace management permission. See [Roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions).
</Note>

## Security and access

By default a space is **open** — everyone in your organization can find it in the sidebar and open the pages inside it. You can restrict a space so that only specific people can reach it, and control what each of those people is allowed to do.

Open a space's **… menu → Security** to manage this, and choose one of:

- **Open** — everyone in the organization can find and open the space. Each person's access comes from their organization role, and you can raise or lower it for specific people.
- **Closed** — only the people you list can open the space. Nobody else can see it or its pages.

Each person on a space has an **access level**:

| Level | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | Open and read the space and its pages |
| **Contributor** | Also edit page content |
| **Admin** | Full control, including changing who can open the space and what each person can do |

Access levels use the same names as [organization roles](/team/roles-and-permissions), and where someone's access could come from more than one source, the highest always applies. Closing a space keeps everyone you named individually and only withdraws the organization-wide access, so reopening it restores the previous state.

<Note>
  Only an **Admin** on a space can change its security or someone's access level. Folders have no security of their own — they are reached through their space, and the pages inside them are [shared individually](/content/pages-and-blocks#sharing-a-page).
</Note>

## How it fits together

Spaces and folders are purely organizational — the real content lives in the [pages](/content/pages-and-blocks) they contain. A typical hierarchy looks like:

- **Space** — "Morning Show"
  - **Folder** — "Week 23"
    - **Page** — "Monday rundown"
    - **Page** — "Tuesday rundown"
