The **NewBlue Captivate** device controls [NewBlue Captivate](https://www.newbluefx.com/captivate/) live titling and graphics, so a rundown item can put the right title on air carrying live data from your page.

## Connection

Provide the Captivate machine's **host** and, if you have changed them in Captivate, its **command** and **notification** ports. Captivate's API has no authentication, so keep it on a trusted studio network.

## Commands

- **Play**, **stop**, **cut in**, **cut out**, or **alert** a title
- Play a title while setting a **title variable**, or update a variable on a title already on air
- Update a variable across every title on a data-controller **input**
- **Load a project** and set the **output device**
- Start and stop **streaming**

Title variables can carry live values from your page, so a lower third updates on cue rather than showing fixed wording.

## State and triggers

The device reports its connection state and raises triggers your [rules](/automation/rules) can react to:

- Captivate **connecting or disconnecting**
- A title's **play state** changing
- A title's **data** changing

Trigger data such as the title, variable, and value is available for use in rule actions.
