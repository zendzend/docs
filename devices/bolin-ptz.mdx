The **Bolin PTZ** device controls [Bolin Technology](https://www.bolintechnology.com/) pan/tilt/zoom cameras — including the R9, EXU, Dante AV, 7 Series, and Blue Line — over VISCA-over-IP.

## Connection

The agent connects to the camera over your network. Provide the camera's **host**, and if needed adjust the **port** (default **52381**), the **camera address** (almost always **1**), and the **status poll interval**.

<Note>
  Bolin cameras control over VISCA on an unauthenticated network — keep them on an isolated production network.
</Note>

## Commands

The Bolin PTZ device supports the core camera operations, including:

- **Pan, tilt, zoom, and focus** — drive in a direction, move to an absolute or relative position, return **home**, or reset; toggle **auto focus** or trigger a one-push focus
- **Presets** — recall, store, or clear a stored position
- **Power** the camera on or to standby
- Adjust **exposure**, **backlight compensation**, and **white balance**, set **iris**, **gain**, and **shutter** (in manual mode), and **flip** or **mirror** the image
- Control the **tally** lamp (on supported models)
- Send a raw command for operations not listed above, including model-specific functions such as wiper or stream control

## State and triggers

The device reports its connection state and raises triggers your [rules](/automation/rules) can react to, including:

- Connection changes
- **Power** on / standby changes
- A **preset** being recalled

Trigger data such as the preset number and power state is available for use in rule actions.
