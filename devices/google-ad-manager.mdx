The **Google Ad Manager** device tells [Google Ad Manager](https://admanager.google.com/) that an ad break is coming in a live stream, giving it time to assemble the ad pod for dynamic ad insertion (DAI).

## Connection

Connect Google Ad Manager from **Automations → Integrations**: choose **Google Ad Manager**, enter your Ad Manager **network code**, and sign in with a Google account that can authorize the network. Once connected, add the device from **Automations → Devices** and select the network.

<Note>
  You need a Google Ad Manager network with dynamic ad insertion and API access enabled, and a live stream event created in Ad Manager to receive the breaks.
</Note>

## Commands

The Google Ad Manager device supports ad-break operations:

- **Trigger an ad break** — schedule a break of a given duration, fired ahead of time so Ad Manager has the lead time it needs. You identify the stream, and can add audience targeting parameters and a pod template.
- **Cancel an ad break** — withdraw a scheduled break before Ad Manager begins assembling its pod.

## State and triggers

The device reports its connection state, the last ad break it created, and that break's current state (scheduled, decisioned, or complete). It raises a trigger your [rules](/automation/rules) can react to when the connection changes.
