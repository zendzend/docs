The **Allen & Heath dLive** device controls an [Allen & Heath dLive](https://www.allenheath.com/hardware/dlive/) mixing system — either the MixRack or the Surface — over the network.

## Connection

The agent connects to the dLive over your local network. Provide the **host** (the MixRack or Surface IP address) and choose the **target** — **MixRack** or **Surface** — which determines the available commands. Set the **base MIDI channel** to match the console's setting. You can enable a **secure (TLS) connection**, in which case you also supply the user profile and password. The common ports are filled in for you and can be overridden.

You can also list the **channels to monitor** so Fabl seeds their state when it connects.

## Commands

The dLive device supports mixing operations, including:

- **Mute** and **unmute** a channel
- Set a channel's **fader level** or a **send level** to an aux, FX, or matrix
- Set a channel's **name** and **colour**
- Assign a channel to the **main mix**, a **DCA**, or a **mute group**
- Adjust **preamp gain**, **pad**, and **phantom power** (MixRack)
- **Recall a scene** (MixRack) or **recall a cue** (Surface)

## State and triggers

The device reports its connection state and raises triggers your [rules](/automation/rules) can react to, including:

- Connection changes
- Channel **mute** changes
- Channel **fader** changes
- **Scene recalled** (MixRack) or **cue recalled** (Surface)

Trigger data such as the channel and level is available for use in rule actions.
