The **Allen & Heath dLive** device controls an [Allen & Heath dLive](https://www.allen-heath.com/hardware/dlive/) digital mixing system, connecting to either the MixRack or the Surface.

## Connection

The agent connects to the console over the network. Provide the **host** and choose whether it connects to the **MixRack** or the **Surface**. If needed, adjust the **port** and the console's **base channel**, and set which **channels** Fabl should monitor.

For a secured connection, enable **TLS** and provide the **user profile** and **password**, along with the certificate-trust option (validate, pin, or trust any) and an optional certificate fingerprint.

## Commands

The dLive device supports common mixing operations, including:

- **Mute** and **unmute** a channel
- Set a channel's **fader level**
- Set a channel's **send level** to an aux, FX, or matrix
- Assign a channel to the **main mix**, a **DCA**, or a **mute group**
- Adjust a mic **preamp** — gain, pad, and 48V phantom power
- **Rename** a channel or change its **strip colour**
- **Recall a scene** (MixRack) or **recall a cue** (Surface)
- Fire **Scene Go**, **Next**, or **Previous** (Surface)

## State and triggers

The device reports its connection state and raises triggers your [rules](/automation/rules) can react to, including:

- Connection changes
- Channel **mute** changes
- Channel **fader** changes
- **Scene** and **cue** recalls

Trigger data such as the channel number and level is available for use in rule actions.
