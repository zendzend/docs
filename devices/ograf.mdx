The **OGraf** device controls any graphics player that implements the [OGraf](https://ograf.ebu.io) graphics API, letting your rundown drive on-air graphics.

## Connection

The agent connects to the graphics player over the network. Provide the **API base URL** and, if the player requires it, an **API key**. If needed, set a **renderer ID** and the **poll interval**.

## Commands

The OGraf device supports graphics operations, including:

- **Show** a graphic on a layer, with its data
- **Update** a graphic's data live
- **Step** a graphic
- **Stop** a graphic
- **Clear** a layer
- Invoke a **custom action** on a graphic or on the renderer

## State and triggers

The device reports its connection state and raises a trigger when the graphics output connects or drops.
