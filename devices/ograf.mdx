The **OGraf** device drives a graphics player that implements the [OGraf](https://ograf.ebu.io/) open graphics standard, letting your rundown put graphics on air through any compatible renderer.

## Connection

The agent connects to the graphics player over your local network. Provide the player's **API base URL** and, if the player requires one, an **API key**. If the player exposes more than one renderer, set the **renderer ID**; otherwise leave it blank. You can adjust how often Fabl reads the renderer with the **poll interval**.

## Commands

The OGraf device supports graphics operations, including:

- **Show** a graphic on a layer with its data
- **Update** a graphic's data live
- **Step** a graphic forward or to a specific step
- **Stop** a graphic with its out animation
- **Clear** a layer
- Run a graphic's **custom action**

## State and triggers

The device reports live renderer state — including which renderer is in use, its status, the available layers and graphics, and which graphics are currently on air. It raises a trigger your [rules](/automation/rules) can react to when the renderer connects or disconnects.
