The **Sony PTZ** devices control [Sony professional PTZ cameras](https://pro.sony/en_US/products/pan-tilt-zoom-cameras) over the network, so a rundown item can frame a shot, recall a preset, or power a camera without an operator touching it.

Fabl offers two Sony camera device types. They control the same cameras in different ways, and you can add either — or both for one camera:

- **Sony VISCA** — the fast, low-latency motion path every Sony PTZ body speaks. Best for pan, tilt, zoom and focus, presets, power and core imaging.
- **Sony HTTP** — Sony's HTTP web interface, available on newer bodies such as the BRC-X400 and SRG-X families. Choose it for the higher-level features that path adds, such as auto-framing, richer imaging control and named presets.

<Tip>
  A Sony camera that isn't listed here still works with the generic [VISCA PTZ](/devices/visca-ptz) device, which carries the same Sony support.
</Tip>

## Supported cameras

Between them the two device types cover the current Sony PTZ line, including the BRC-X400 and X401, the SRG-X400, X402 and X120, the SRG-A and SRG-H series, and the ILME-FR7. Sony HTTP targets the models that expose Sony's web interface — the BRC-X400 and SRG-X generation.

## Connection

### Sony VISCA

Provide the camera's **host**. The **port** and **camera address** (almost always **1** over IP) can be left at their defaults, as can the **status poll interval**.

<Note>
  VISCA over IP is switched **off** by default on many Sony bodies and must be enabled first — through the camera's on-screen menu or its rear switches. The ILME-FR7 needs its switch 3 on, followed by a restart. Until it's enabled the camera won't connect.
</Note>

### Sony HTTP

Provide the camera's **host**, and the **user name** and **password** — modern Sony bodies require them, and force the password to be changed at first setup. **Port**, **status poll interval** and **Model** can be left at their defaults; leave **Model** blank and Fabl detects the camera itself.

## Commands

Both device types support the core camera operations, including:

- **Pan and tilt** — drive in a direction, move to an absolute or relative position, stop, or return **home**
- **Zoom** — drive in or out, or move to an absolute position
- **Focus** — drive near or far, set an absolute position, or toggle **auto focus**
- **Presets** — recall or store a position
- **Power** the camera on or to standby
- **White balance** and **image stabilizer**

Sony HTTP additionally reaches Sony's higher-level imaging and auto-framing features, and can name the presets it stores. On both device types, a raw command is available for operations not listed above.

## Triggers

[Rules](/automation/rules) can react to:

- The camera connection changes
- The camera powers on or goes to standby
- A preset is recalled

<Tip>
  Recalling a preset raises a trigger carrying the preset number, so one rule can react to "any preset recalled" and branch on which one it was.
</Tip>

## State

Each device reports its connection state, power state, the last preset recalled, and live pan, tilt, zoom and focus positions.
