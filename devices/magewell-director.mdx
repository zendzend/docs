The **Magewell Director** device controls a [Magewell Director](https://www.magewell.com/director) live production unit — recording, streaming, scene switching, graphics overlays, and its scoreboard, countdown, and timer.

## Connection

The agent connects to the Director over the network. Provide the **host** and select the **model**. If needed, choose the **control transport** (HTTP or OSC) and adjust its **port**.

## Commands

The Director device supports live-production operations, including:

- Start and stop **recording**
- Start and stop **streaming**, and **stop all streaming**
- **Switch scene**, with a transition
- **Show** or **hide** a graphics overlay, and **clear** overlays
- **Set** or **adjust** a team's score, and **reset the scoreboard**
- **Set a countdown**, and **show** or **hide** it
- Control a **timer** — play, pause, or reset

## State

The device reports its connection state. This driver sends commands to the Director and does not raise triggers.
