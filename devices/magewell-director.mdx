The **Magewell Director** device controls a [Magewell Director](https://www.magewell.com/director) live production switcher (Director Mini, One, or Plus) over the network.

## Connection

The agent connects to the Director over your local network. Provide the **host** (the Director's IP address) and choose the **model**. The default **transport** and **port** suit most setups and can be overridden.

## Commands

The Magewell Director device supports live production operations, including:

- **Switch a scene** to programme, with a cut or fade
- **Start** and **stop** streaming, and stop all streams at once
- **Start** and **stop** recording
- **Show**, **hide**, and **clear** graphics overlays
- **Set** and **adjust** a scoreboard score, and **reset** the scoreboard
- Set, show, and hide a **countdown timer**, and **play, pause, or reset** a timer

## State

The device reports its connection state and the outcome of the last command. This driver sends commands to the Director and does not raise triggers.
