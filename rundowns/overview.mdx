Rundowns are the backbone of every production in Fabl. They give your entire team — producers, directors, talent, and crew — a live, shared view of the show's structure, timing, and content. Whether you're producing a daily live broadcast, a recorded series, or a one-off event, rundowns keep everyone aligned from the first planning session through the final wrap.

## What Is a Rundown?

A rundown is a structured document that breaks your production into ordered **segments**, each with a title, type, duration, notes, and optional cue points or linked shot lists. As you build and adjust the rundown, Fabl automatically calculates total running time and flags any overages — so you never walk into a broadcast unprepared.

## When to Use Rundowns

Rundowns are the right tool any time your production has a defined structure and a clock to hit. Common use cases include:

- **Live broadcasts and streams** — keep hosts, directors, and technical crews locked to the same clock
- **Pre-recorded shows** — plan segments, b-roll windows, and ad breaks before the shoot
- **Corporate events and productions** — share a single agenda that updates in real time as logistics shift
- **Daily news and magazine shows** — manage recurring segment types (intros, breaks, outros, ads) with consistent templates

## Key Capabilities

- **Automatic timing calculations** — set durations per segment and Fabl totals running time instantly
- **Real-time collaboration** — multiple team members can view and edit the same rundown simultaneously
- **Version history and rollback** — every change is saved so you can review history or restore a previous version
- **Segment-level detail** — attach notes, cue points, and shot lists directly to individual segments
- **Flexible export** — share the final rundown as a PDF, Excel file, or a print-ready layout
