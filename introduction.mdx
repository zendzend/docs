Fabl is a broadcast production platform for live and studio teams. It brings together a collaborative, block-based content workspace and real-time control of your studio hardware — so the same rundown your producers build is the rundown that drives your switcher, graphics, audio, lighting, and teleprompter on air.

## What Fabl does

You build your production as **pages** made of reusable **blocks**, run any page as a live **rundown** from a studio control surface, and let an on-premises **agent** translate your cues into commands for the gear in your gallery.

## Who Fabl is for

Fabl is built for the people who plan and run live productions:

- **Producers** who build rundowns and need them to stay in sync across the whole team during a show
- **Directors and technical directors** who cue and take items live and want the gear to follow the rundown
- **Operators** who run switchers, graphics, audio, and lighting and need reliable, repeatable commands
- **Media teams** who manage the video, captions, and graphics that feed the show

## How Fabl is organized

Your team works inside a **workspace**. Content is organized into **spaces** and **folders** that hold **pages**, and pages are built from **blocks**. When it's showtime, you run a page in **studio mode** as a rundown, and a connected **agent** relays your cues to your **devices**. See [Key concepts](/key-concepts) for the full breakdown.
