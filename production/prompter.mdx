The **prompter** is a full-screen, chrome-free teleprompter view of a [page](/content/pages-and-blocks). It's designed for talent: large type, distraction-free, and able to follow the show in real time.

## Open the prompter

Open the prompter for a page to launch its dedicated full-screen view. The toolbar hides itself after a few seconds and reappears when you move the mouse to the top of the screen. Because the prompter has its own URL, you can open it on a separate prompter machine or display.

## Controls

- **Play / pause scroll** — start or stop auto-scrolling
- **Speed** — set the auto-scroll speed
- **Font size** — scale the text up or down for your distance
- **Color scheme** — choose dark, light, or a **custom** background and text color
- **Mirror** — flip the text horizontally for use with a beam-splitter prompter glass
- **Back to top** — stop and jump to the start
- **Overview** — open a panel to jump to any block on the page

## Voice-controlled scrolling

The prompter can scroll in time with the talent's delivery. Enable **speech scroll** and the prompter listens through the microphone and advances the text as words are spoken, keeping the read position under the talent's pace without a manual operator.

## Driven by your rundown

During a live show, [automation rules](/automation/rules) can control the prompter as you advance the [rundown](/production/rundowns). Rules can send a block's content to the prompter, clear it, reset it, focus a specific field, or start scrolling — so the right script appears automatically as each item goes on air.
