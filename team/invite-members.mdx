Fabl is built for teams. You invite teammates to your [workspace](/key-concepts) by email, and they join as soon as they accept.

## Invite teammates

When you first set up Fabl, the onboarding flow includes an **invite teammates** step:

1. Enter a teammate's **email address**.
2. Choose a **role** for them — **Member** or **Admin**.
3. Add more rows for additional people, then click **Send invites**.

Each person receives an email invitation with a link to join your workspace. You can also choose **Skip for now** and invite people later.

## Roles at a glance

- **Admin** — full control of the workspace, including managing other members
- **Member** — access governed by the granular permissions assigned to them

What each member can see and do is controlled by permissions. See [Roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions) for the full model.

<Note>
  Inviting and managing members is an administrative action. Members without management permissions can use the workspace but can't change its membership.
</Note>
